Before you can publish a documentation site, you need a Mintlify account and a workspace. This page walks you through signing up, creating your workspace, bringing in your teammates, and reviewing your plan options — so your whole team is ready to start writing and shipping docs.

<Tip>
  Mintlify's free plan includes unlimited pages, GitHub integration, and AI-powered search. It's the best way to get started — no credit card required.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to mintlify.com and click Sign Up">
    Open [mintlify.com](https://mintlify.com) in your browser and click the **Get Started** button in the top navigation. This takes you to the sign-up page where you can create a new account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in with GitHub or email">
    Choose your preferred sign-in method:

    - **GitHub** — Recommended. Signing in with GitHub makes it faster to connect your repositories later, since Mintlify can request the necessary permissions in a single step.
    - **Email** — Enter your work email and follow the verification link sent to your inbox.

    Once authenticated, you'll land on the workspace creation screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    Every Mintlify account is organized around one or more **workspaces**. A workspace groups your documentation sites, team members, and billing together.

    Fill in the following fields:

    | Field | Description |
    |-------|-------------|
    | **Workspace Name** | The display name shown in your dashboard (e.g., "Acme Docs"). |
    | **Workspace Slug** | A lowercase, URL-safe identifier used in your default subdomain. |

    <Info>
      Your workspace slug becomes the subdomain of your default Mintlify-hosted URL: `your-slug.mintlify.app`. Choose carefully — changing it later updates your live URL, which may break existing links.
    </Info>

    Click **Create Workspace** to continue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite team members">
    To bring teammates into your workspace, go to **Settings → Team** in the left sidebar. Enter your teammate's email address and assign them a role:

    - **Owner** — Full access including billing and workspace deletion.
    - **Editor** — Can create, edit, and delete documentation content and settings.
    - **Viewer** — Read-only access to the dashboard (useful for stakeholders).

    Each invited teammate receives an email with a link to accept the invitation. They'll be added to your workspace as soon as they sign in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review billing and plan options">
    Navigate to **Settings → Billing** to review the available plans. Mintlify offers tiered plans based on the number of editors, custom domains, advanced analytics, and priority support.

    You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from the Billing page. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>
